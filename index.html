Dear Mishi, 💗

I don't really know how to fit everything into a birthday message... because some people are simply too special to fit into a few lines.

But today is your day, so I had to try.

4 September 2008...

The day a girl named Maisha entered this world. And somehow, she became this wonderfully random combination of things...

📚 A girl who can disappear into a book and forget about the rest of the world.
🍦 A girl who believes ice cream can make almost any day better.
👩‍🍳 A girl who can turn a kitchen into her own little kingdom.
🎨 A girl who can take an empty canvas and turn it into something beautiful.
👻 And, of course... a girl who can be completely defeated by the existence of a ghost. 😂

Honestly... I think those little things are what make you, you. Your interests. Your little habits. Your painting. Your funny fears. Your smile. Your way of being yourself.

And then there's your painting... you can take a blank canvas and somehow turn it into something that feels alive. I think that's a pretty amazing thing to be able to do. Books, cooking, painting... you have this funny little habit of turning ordinary things into something special.

I hope you never lose those little things. Keep reading the stories you love. Keep painting your world. Keep creating delicious things. Keep finding happiness in the simplest moments.

And whenever life gets difficult... remember that one difficult page doesn't mean the whole story is bad. There are still so many chapters waiting for you.

I hope the chapters ahead are filled with: more laughter, more adventures, more books, more ice cream, more beautiful paintings, more delicious creations, more happy memories... and absolutely ZERO ghosts. 👻😭

And maybe... a few unexpected people who make your days a little brighter. 🌷

Happy Birthday, Mishi. 🎂💗

I hope this new chapter becomes one of your most beautiful ones yet. Keep smiling. Keep being you. And never forget... you are worth celebrating.

With lots of warmth & care,
Someone who wanted to make your birthday a little more special. ✨
